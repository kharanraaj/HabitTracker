Privacy & Local Storage Architecture

Zero external services. 100% private to your browser.
Local Only

All habits, entries, notes, and settings are stored strictly in your browser’s local storage. No information is ever transmitted to any third-party server, tracking service, or cloud AI.

Important Note: Because data is stored in the browser, clearing browsing cache/site data or using incognito sessions can erase your local history. Please create regular JSON backup exports.
